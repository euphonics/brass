# Scribidian 0.1.2

Requires Obsidian 1.13.7 or later. Still a beta: keep backups of your
screenplays, and please
[report problems](https://github.com/euphonics/scribidian/issues).

## Highlights

- **Typing is fast again in long scripts.** In a 500-page script,
  Scribidian on its own now types about as fast as a plain Obsidian note:
  about 1 ms of its own per keystroke, down from 43 ms in 0.1.1, with no
  more pauses over 50 ms (25 in a short burst before). Enter and Tab are
  two to three times faster (Enter 101 → 37 ms). Counts and statistics run
  in the background.
- **The PDF looks like Fade In's**: the same margins, page numbers, title
  page, dual dialogue side by side, scene numbers, page breaks (`===`),
  speeches split across pages with (MORE), and **(CONT'D)** added on the
  page (never in your text). A bold switch for each part of the page.
- **Clean export for every format.** Export clean Fountain, clean PDF and
  clean Final Draft, each with its own column in **Settings → Scribidian →
  Clean export**. The clean Fountain file keeps Fountain's own hidden
  elements (sections, synopses, boneyard) and can turn `%%` and `//`
  comments into Fountain notes or boneyard. Wikilinks keep their alias or
  file name; web links their name or address.
- **Whole sluglines in one pick**: picking `STUDIO, BARN - NIGHT
(FLASHBACK)` in the slugline list fills in all of it. **Picking a
  location fills in** (Suggestions) has Time and Modifier checkboxes.
- **Character groups**: `+jen` also offers `Jen and Blake` and
  `Jen, Blake, and Helios` from the scene, and finishes a list as you type
  it (`+Jen, Blake, and H`).
- **Toggle dual dialogue**: a command that adds or removes the `^` on the
  speech you're in.
- **Threads panel** works like Obsidian's search: open and close each
  thread and note.
- **Mini sluglines** (`.HALL`) can be picked without a time.
- Many smaller fixes; the full list is in the
  [changelog](https://github.com/euphonics/scribidian/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Changed behavior

- **Export PDF** now keeps Obsidian markup as text, like **Export
  Fountain**. Use **Export clean PDF** (or the preview's button) for the
  PDF you're used to.
- **Picking a location** fills in its last time and modifier. To get the
  old behavior (the location, then the times list), turn off both
  checkboxes under **Picking a location fills in**.
- Standard character extensions (V.O., O.S.) come before the ones your
  script uses; **Your extensions first** (Suggestions) puts yours first.

## Updating

BRAT updates Scribidian on its own, or choose **Check for updates** in
BRAT's settings. To update by hand, replace `main.js`, `manifest.json` and
`styles.css` in `.obsidian/plugins/scribidian/` with the files below.

Your settings are kept. Your clean export choices carry over to all three
formats (Fountain, PDF, Final Draft); the PDF and Final Draft columns then
leave out what those formats can't hold.
