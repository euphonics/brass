# Scribidian 0.1.3

Requires Obsidian 1.13.7 or later. Still a beta: keep backups of your
screenplays, and please
[report problems](https://github.com/euphonics/scribidian/issues).

## Highlights

- **Link scenes and outline, both ways.** The command **Link scene
  headings across documents** links each scene to its heading in your
  outline (`= [[Outline#Heading]]` under the scene's heading, or under
  each slugline: a setting), and adds a link back from the outline to the
  scene. Scenes match outline headings by name; for the rest, pick one or
  skip.
- **Move boneyard to the boneyard note.** Every `/* ... */` block leaves
  the script and lands in the screenplay's boneyard note, under a dated
  heading that links back to where it was.
  **Move selection to the boneyard note** sends any selected text there,
  and **Wrap selection in boneyard** adds or removes `/* */`.
- **Outlines and notes share the script's helpers.** Notes in the
  project's folder (or named in the script's `outline` property) get `+`
  character names and the script's `$` variables, and `$` variables are
  shared across the whole project folder.
- **Embeds in exports.** Clean export's **Embeds** can now be **Include
  text**: the embedded note, section or block goes into the export and
  preview, cleaned like the script. (Images come later.)
- **Shift+Tab goes back a part** in a slugline or character, opening that
  part's list (`INT. BARN - DAY|` → DAY → BARN).
- **Looser `+` names**: `+Jenandb` finds `Jen and Blake`; `+j,b` finds
  `Jen, Blake, and George`.
- **Threads show their names** (`🧵black widow Fiona`); `[&gun|the
pistol]` shows and exports its own text, and `[&gun|]` shows just the
  spool.
- **Turn off Completr in screenplays** (on by default), so its word list
  doesn't cover Scribidian's.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/euphonics/scribidian/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Updating

BRAT updates Scribidian on its own, or choose **Check for updates** in
BRAT's settings. To update by hand, replace `main.js`, `manifest.json` and
`styles.css` in `.obsidian/plugins/scribidian/` with the files below.
Your settings are kept.
