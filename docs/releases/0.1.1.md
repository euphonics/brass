# Scribidian 0.1.1

Requires Obsidian 1.13.7 or later. Still a beta: keep backups of your
screenplays, and please
[report problems](https://github.com/euphonics/scribidian/issues).

## Highlights

- **Character names finish themselves in action lines.** Type a name's
  first letters in capitals (`MARG`) to get `MARGARET`, or `+marg` to get
  `Margaret`. Now on by default.
- **PDF export is back**, with Courier Prime built in. It prints what clean
  Fountain export keeps, so your notes stay out.
- **Clean export takes out all Obsidian markup by default**: links, embeds,
  tags, tasks, callouts, highlights and comments. The page count ignores
  them too.
- **Placeholders**: type `$villain` for a name you haven't chosen. It is
  highlighted and suggested each time you type `$`.
- **Threads**: tie a setup to its payoff with `[&name]` and jump between
  them; **Show threads** lists every place.
- **Smarter sluglines**: places come back with the time of day you used
  last (`KITCHEN - NIGHT`), and you can turn on whole-slug suggestions and
  choose their order.
- **Sentences start with a capital** as you type (script mode; a setting).
- **Many Tab and Enter fixes and settings**, several matching Fade In, such
  as **Enter at the end of a character moves to the dialogue**.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/euphonics/scribidian/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Updating

BRAT updates Scribidian on its own, or choose **Check for updates** in
BRAT's settings. To update by hand, replace `main.js`, `manifest.json` and
`styles.css` in `.obsidian/plugins/scribidian/` with the files below.

Your settings are kept. Settings you saved before keep their values, so
**Suggest character names in action** and clean export's **Remove tags**
stay as they were if you had set them; turn them on in Settings →
Scribidian if you want the new defaults.
